1. The service

Ringkit is business phone infrastructure. We give you phone numbers, answer and route your calls, and turn each call into a summary. An AI model triages every inbound call and sends it to a bot, to your own team, or to a Ringkit rep, based on rules you set. You can use Ringkit from the dashboard or build on it through our API.

Some features are labeled beta. Beta features may change or be removed, and they don't carry the commitments these terms make for generally available features.

2. Accounts

You need an account to use Ringkit. You must be at least 18 and able to enter into a contract. If you sign up on behalf of a business, you confirm you're authorized to accept these terms for it.

Keep your login and API keys secure. You're responsible for activity under your account, including calls placed by apps or agents using your keys. If you think a key or login has been compromised, rotate the key and tell us right away.

The information you give us — your business details and the brief we build from your site and connected tools — should be accurate. The AI and our reps rely on it when they speak for you.

3. Pay-per-minute billing

Ringkit has no platform fee and no minimum. You pay for the numbers you keep and the minutes you use:

AI answering$0.05 / min

  • Each call is billed per minute, rounded up to the next full minute. A call lasting 4 minutes 10 seconds is billed as 5 minutes.
  • For example, a 5-minute AI-handled call costs $0.25, and a 6-minute quote call handled by a live rep costs $3.00.
  • Usage is billed monthly in arrears to the payment method on file. Your invoice lists every call, its duration and how it was handled.
  • The number fee applies for each month, or part of a month, that a number is on your account.
  • Prices don't include taxes. Where taxes or regulatory telecom fees apply, they're shown separately on your invoice.

There's no contract and no term commitment. You can cancel anytime; you'll get a final invoice for usage up to the moment you cancel. If we change prices, we'll email you at least 30 days before the change applies.

If a payment fails, we'll let you know and retry. If a balance stays unpaid, we may pause service until it's settled.

5. Call recording & consent

Calls are recorded and transcribed to make the summary. Ringkit plays a consent notice on every call and follows two-party-consent rules by state.

That notice is a baseline, not a substitute for your own obligations. Some states, such as California, require every party to consent before a call is recorded. You're responsible for making sure recording is lawful for your business and your callers, including any additional notices your industry requires.

6. Ringkit reps

When a call routes to a Ringkit rep, that rep acts as your front desk. Before the call they're briefed on your business — your services, service area, rates and how you want calls handled — and they follow that brief.

  • Reps answer in your business's name, take messages, qualify leads, book appointments and share information you've approved.
  • Reps don't make binding commitments for you — prices, discounts, warranties, schedules or contract terms — beyond what you've authorized in your brief.
  • If a caller asks for something outside the brief, the rep takes the details and flags it in the call summary for you to follow up.

You're responsible for keeping your brief accurate and current. Reps work for Ringkit, not for you, and they see only your brief and the live call.

7. Numbers & porting

Numbers you get from Ringkit stay assigned to your account while it's active. Phone numbers are regulated, and in rare cases we may have to change or reclaim one if a carrier or regulator requires it. We'll tell you as early as we can if that happens.

Keep your existing number. Porting is free and takes 3–10 business days with most US carriers; your old line keeps working until the port completes.

If you leave Ringkit, you can port your numbers to another provider. We'll release them promptly once we receive a valid port request.

8. Availability

Calls run on redundant carrier routes in multiple US regions. Status page and incident history are public.

At launch, Ringkit doesn't offer an uptime guarantee or service credits. Phone service depends on carriers and other providers outside our control, and outages can happen; check the status page for current incidents. Ringkit isn't a replacement for emergency services — don't rely on it to place or receive 911 calls.

9. Termination

Before you close, you can export your call history, recordings and summaries. After closure, we delete your data as described in our Privacy Policy.
