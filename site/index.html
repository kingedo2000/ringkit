HUMANS IN THE LOOP, ON PURPOSE

Bots for the easy calls, people for the ones that matter.

Bot-only answering services put every caller through the same script — including the customer about to hand you a remodel. Ringkit lets AI take the hours-and-directions calls and puts a briefed person on the quote, the complaint and the new job.

It isn't "replace your front desk with a robot." It's a front desk that knows your business, available the minute you need it.

  • Our reps or yours — your call

    Send calls to Ringkit's on-demand reps, to your own team, or a mix. Change it any time.

  • Briefed before they pick up

    Your services, pricing, hours and open jobs are on screen before the first hello. Live reps are $0.50/min, billed only when we answer.

  • Your best people only take the calls that matter

    Routine calls never reach you or your senior people, so you're free when the big job calls.

TRUST

Security & compliance

Recording & consent

Calls are recorded and transcribed to make the summary. Ringkit plays a consent notice on every call and follows two-party-consent rules by state.

Data handling

Your business brief, call recordings and summaries are encrypted at rest and in transit, and never used to train models for anyone else.

Number porting

Keep your existing number. Porting is free and takes 3–10 business days with most US carriers; your old line keeps working until the port completes.

Reliability

Calls run on redundant carrier routes in multiple US regions. Status page and incident history are public.
