Billing

How billing works

One invoice a month, itemized by call. Bots for the easy calls, people for the ones that matter — and you only pay the people rate when a person is on the line.

  1. 01
    Per minute, rounded up
    Each call is billed by the minute, rounded up to the next minute. A 2:10 call is 3 minutes.
  2. 02
    Billed monthly in arrears
    You're charged at the end of each month for the minutes you actually used.
  3. 03
    No minimums
    A quiet month costs $1 for the number. No platform fee, no seat fees.
  4. 04
    Cancel anytime
    No contracts. Release or port out your number whenever you like.
  5. 05
    AI minutes vs. rep minutes
    A call that starts with AI and transfers to a Ringkit rep is billed at each rate for the minutes each handled. Two AI minutes then six rep minutes = $0.10 + $3.00.
FAQ

Questions about pricing

Do I need to port my number?

No. You can take a new $1 number, forward your existing line to Ringkit, or port it in. Keep your existing number: porting is free and takes 3–10 business days with most US carriers; your old line keeps working until the port completes.

Who are the reps?

Trained Ringkit phone reps. Before a call reaches them, the AI hands over your business brief and what the caller has said so far, so they pick up already knowing your services, rates and service area.

What happens after hours?

You set the rules per number: AI-only after hours, a Ringkit rep for anything urgent, or voicemail. Every call still ends up as a summary waiting for you in the morning.

Can I use my own team only?

Yes. The AI can route calls to your own people by tier and never hand off to a Ringkit rep. The $0.50/min live-rep rate only applies to minutes a Ringkit rep handles.

Is there a free trial?

Sandbox numbers are free for testing; see the docs.

Do you record calls?

Calls are recorded and transcribed to make the summary. Ringkit plays a consent notice on every call and follows two-party-consent rules by state.
